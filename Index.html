Introduction BIRTHDAY FRIENDS APP ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and your rights when you use our Android mobile application. 2. Information We Collect We collect only the information needed to provide the core features of the App: Profile Information: Full Name, State, Date of Birth (required for birthday reminders), and Profile Photo (optional). Unique User ID: An automatically generated User ID used to identify your profile without requiring email, phone number, or password login for standard users. Messages & Gift Cards: Text messages and virtual birthday gift cards you choose to send in private one-to-one chats. Contacts Permission (Optional): If you grant the READ_CONTACTS permission, the App accesses your contacts strictly on your device so you can select friends to invite via SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram. Your contact list is never uploaded to or stored on external servers. Notification Permission (Optional): Used to notify you about birthdays, new messages, and gift cards if you enable notifications. Device & Activity Information: A device identifier (to support one profile per device and prevent duplicate accounts) and your last active timestamp (to manage inactive accounts). 3. How We Use Your Information Display your profile to active users and calculate today's and upcoming birthdays according to Indian Standard Time (IST). Deliver private one-to-one text messages and birthday gift cards between participating users. Maintain your Favourite Friends list and verify friend invite rewards. Maintain app security, prevent abuse or duplicate profiles, and handle user reports. 4. Private Chat & Security One-to-One Chat Isolation: Private text messages and gift cards shared between two users are visible only to those two participants. Other users cannot view your private chat history, chat list, or participants. Screenshot Protection: When viewing another user's profile, the App uses Android's FLAG_SECURE setting to help discourage unauthorized screenshots and screen recording. Encrypted Transit: Online data transfers with backend and advertising services use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. Logout: Logging out returns you to the Welcome Page without deleting your saved profile or data. 5.
